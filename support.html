Quick Troubleshooting

  • Camera permission denied: Open iOS Settings → Trouvaille → enable Camera & Photos access, then relaunch Capture.
  • Exporting at 2K High-Res: Open Profile → Camera & appearance → Export quality and switch from 1080p to 2K for 2160px gallery prints.
  • Layering 2 presets at once: Open Capture → Settings (or Profile) and turn Dual Preset to 2×.
  • Silent shooting: Tap the speaker icon in Capture → Settings or switch Shutter & dial sound to Silent in Profile.
