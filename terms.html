App license

Trouvaille for iPhone is licensed under the Apple Standard End User License Agreement (Terms of Use).

Trouvaille Pro

Pro adds Dual Preset and full-resolution photo export. It is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase. Apple shows the price and billing period before you confirm a purchase and charges your Apple Account.

Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Apple may charge for renewal in the 24 hours before the new billing period begins. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Subscriptions. Deleting your Trouvaille account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

To restore an eligible purchase, open Profile → Trouvaille Pro → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Submit eligible refund requests through Apple.

Photos and shared recipes

You retain ownership of your photos. Import and share only content you have permission to use. If you publish a recipe, its settings and profile attribution can be viewed through its shared link. Your remix setting determines whether others can create a remix from your recipe in Trouvaille.
