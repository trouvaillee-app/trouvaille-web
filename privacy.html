PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED NOVEMBER 20, 2026

Your photographs stay yours.

Trouvaille, part of Syntheticae Group, processes your photographs on your device. This policy explains what we collect when you use an account, purchase Pro, or contact us.

Photos and permissions

Capture, editing, and composition run locally. Trouvaille stores originals, edits, and compositions in your local Roll and does not upload them to its servers. If you export a photo or composition, you choose where to save it or which app to share it with.

The app uses your camera for the live viewfinder and capture, the system photo picker when you import an image, and photo permissions when needed to save to Photos. The level guide uses device orientation when available. You can change those permissions in iOS Settings. Camera footage and orientation readings are processed on the device.

Accounts

You can shoot, import, develop, compose, and export at standard quality without an account. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a provider identifier and the email address and name the provider makes available. Apple can provide a relay email address if you choose Hide My Email. For accounts provisioned with email sign-in, we process the email address and credentials through our authentication provider. We use account information to authenticate you, maintain your account, and create a profile with a display name and handle.

Saved looks and film settings are stored locally with your photos.

Purchases and support

Apple processes in-app payments. RevenueCat receives your Trouvaille account ID and App Store purchase and subscription information to verify purchases, unlock Pro, and restore access. Trouvaille does not receive your card details.

If you email us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send to respond and resolve your request. Hosting and app services may process network information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect their services.

Service providers

We use Supabase to authenticate accounts and store profiles. We use RevenueCat to confirm Pro access. We require providers that process data on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable privacy requirements. Apple and Google apply their own privacy policies when you use their sign-in services. Apple also applies its privacy policy to App Store purchases.

Retention and deletion

Photos and compositions remain in your local Roll until you remove them. To remove copies you exported, delete them where you saved or shared them. Account records remain while your account is active.

To remove your account and profile from Trouvaille, sign in, open Profile → Account → Delete Account, and confirm. Local items in Roll and copies you exported are managed separately. Deleting your Trouvaille account does not cancel an Apple subscription. You can manage it in Apple Subscriptions.

We retain support correspondence while needed to respond and follow up. Purchase records or other information may be kept as needed for billing, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or dispute resolution. Apple manages App Store purchase records independently.

You can withdraw permissions in iOS Settings or manage sign-in access in your Apple or Google account. To ask about your data or get help with deletion, email trouvaillee.app@gmail.com.
